# NNNN: Short decision title

Status: Proposed

## Context

What problem or constraint requires a decision? Why will the choice matter later?

## Decision

What do we choose, and why? State the scope clearly.

## Alternatives

Which credible alternatives did we consider, and what trade-offs led us away from them?

## Consequences

What does this enable, cost, or constrain? Include compatibility or migration implications where relevant.
